# National Curriculum AI Futures

An interactive guide to how AI affects each subject in England's national curriculum: the knowledge and skills taught, the risks and opportunities AI brings, and whether those skills are likely to be needed, augmented or replaced at roughly 10, 20 and 30 years.

**Live site:** https://inanded.github.io/national-curriculum-ai-futures/

- `index.html`: the interactive site (single file, no build step)
- `report.md`: the full written research report, with a source link for every claim

Research covers evidence available to October 2026. Outlooks beyond about 2036 are scenarios, not forecasts. Figures marked CHECK (or † in the report) were not re-checked against the primary source and should be verified before quoting.
